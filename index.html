LumaEdit Support

Get help with LumaEdit for iPhone and iPad.

Import and export

Use the photo picker to open a photo. After editing, tap Export to save a copy, then choose Share to send it to another app or save it to Files.

LumaEdit Pro

To recover access on a device using the same Apple Account, open LumaEdit Pro and tap Restore Purchases. To change or cancel your subscription, go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Support requests are public. Keep personal photos and account details private.
