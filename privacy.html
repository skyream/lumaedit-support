PhotoLuma Privacy Policy

This policy describes how PhotoLuma handles information when you use the iOS app.

Photos and edits

With your permission, PhotoLuma displays photos from your photo library in its gallery. You can allow access to selected photos or your full library, and change that access in iOS Settings. Without library access, you can still choose an individual photo through the system photo picker. Photos are edited on your device and are not uploaded to PhotoLuma servers. iCloud photos may be downloaded through Apple's Photos service when you browse or open them. Exported files may be kept in the app’s local storage. When you share an export, the destination you choose receives a copy and handles it under its own policies.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. PhotoLuma uses StoreKit to check whether Pro access is active and to restore purchases. Apple handles your billing details under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support and website

GitHub hosts these pages. If you open a support request, your GitHub profile and the information you choose to post may be public. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for how GitHub handles that information.

Managing your information

You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device. Copies you exported or shared can be removed from the places where you saved them. For questions about this policy, use the contact link on the support page.
